It reads the photo first

PixelForge measures light, focus, grain, resolution and whether there's a face — so it only attempts repairs the photo actually needs.

It tries many repairs at once

Brightening, sharpening, denoising, upscaling, colour correction and face repair each produce a candidate — then compete on merit.

You get the winner only

Every candidate is graded for quality and checked so it still looks like your photo. The single best result is what you download.
